# Selected task and approach

We selected UPAR 2027 Track 1 pedestrian attribute recognition: predicting 40 ordered attributes describing age, gender, hair, clothing and accessories from person crops. The official source data contains PA100K, PETA and Market1501, with 97,669 training and 33,407 validation rows. Unknown labels are masked, original images remain read-only, and known exact-pixel train/validation overlaps are excluded from later selection/calibration.

Our original approach uses a general-purpose SigLIP2-Base-NaFlex backbone with semantic attribute queries, patch attention and a global visual residual. It uses masked balanced BCE, square-root-domain sampling and source-validation selection/calibration, then exports native offline torch inference. It is the only officially scored entry: submission 1003877, Challenge_Avg 0.770450.

To investigate the gap between known-domain validation and official performance, we ran three strict leave-one-domain-out folds and one controlled equal-domain sampling comparison. We then tested DINOv3 with six global/regional descriptors, attribute-specific attention and a fixed 75/25 raw-probability ensemble, followed by conservative last-two-block adaptation. A separate bounded surveillance-augmentation branch tested robustness in the original SigLIP architecture. Checkpoint choice and calibration used source data only; no hidden-score parameter search was conducted.

The strongest local transfer candidate was the partially fine-tuned ensemble, macro 0.802508 / worst domain 0.781356. Equal-domain sampling and the robustness branch were rejected. The original frozen and later adapted ensemble gates are preserved as failed; separately authorized exploratory all-domain A/B candidates were packaged. An attempted follow-up upload was interrupted during the reported site outage; neither later ensemble has a confirmed official score. Both improved every local held-out domain, supporting our expectation of better generalization if officially evaluated. The three-page report and complete decision record explain this progression and its limitations.
